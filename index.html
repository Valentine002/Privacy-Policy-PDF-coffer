Privacy Policy

📁 PDF Coffer 📅 Last updated: August 30, 2026
PDF Coffer (“the app”) is a comprehensive PDF utility suite. It provides a variety of tools to view, convert, edit, optimize, and manage your documents entirely on your device.
Your privacy is our priority. This policy explains how PDF Coffer handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

PDF Coffer does not collect, store, or share any personal information or user data.

The app does not require you to:

  • Create an account or register
  • Provide your name, email address, or phone number
  • Grant access to your contacts, location, or personal media (beyond selecting files for processing)
  • Submit any personal information to use the app
🔒 zero data collection We do not track your activity, and we do not have servers that store your files or personal information.

2. Data Processing & Local Tools

All tools within PDF Coffer are designed to work locally on your device. This includes:

View PDF Documents Merge PDF Split PDF Image to PDF PDF to Image Compress PDF Protect PDF Rotate PDF Add Watermark Extract Text ZIP Files View Text View Image

When you use these tools, your files are processed directly on your device. No files, documents, or data are uploaded to us or any third party.

3. Internet Connection & Office Conversion

🌐 requires internet Office Document Conversion: The feature to convert Office documents (such as DOCX, XLSX, PPTX) to PDF requires an active internet connection.

To perform this specific conversion, your document is securely transmitted to a conversion service. This service processes the file solely to complete the conversion and returns the PDF to your device. The file is not stored, viewed, shared, or collected by us or the service after the conversion is complete.

If you choose to view an Office document as text only, that process is handled locally on your device without requiring an internet connection.

4. Permissions

PDF Coffer may request access to your device's file storage. This permission is required solely to allow you to select the files you wish to view, convert, merge, or edit. We do not scan, read, or collect information from your file system beyond the files you explicitly choose to process.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties because we do not collect your data.

6. Children's Privacy

PDF Coffer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. The app is safe for all ages as it does not require registration or the submission of personal details.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of PDF Coffer changes or if necessary to reflect updates in data handling practices. Any changes will be posted on this page.
